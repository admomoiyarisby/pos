# Mission: Omoiyari POS Supply Chain Flow

## Why

Learn how the implemented supply-chain mechanisms actually move documents, stock, and money across Central Warehouse and branch outlets, so product and engineering decisions can be made from the real system behavior.

## Success looks like

- Explain the PR to Surat Jalan to Invoice flow without mixing up requested, picked, received, rejected, and invoiced quantities.
- Predict which database tables change when stock is shipped, received, rejected, transferred, or reordered.
- Spot ambiguous or mismatched FRD versus implementation behavior and make a defensible deduction.

## Constraints

- Ground lessons in the local FRD and current codebase first.
- Keep each lesson short and practical.

## Out of scope

- Full accounting treatment outside SCM invoices.
- Non-supply-chain POS sales behavior except where it affects inventory.
